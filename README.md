## Featured projects

### Bank Campaign Targeting System

End-to-end machine learning project for prioritizing bank marketing campaign targets.

Instead of treating the model as a simple yes/no classifier, this project ranks customers by predicted subscription likelihood and evaluates which top percentage of customers should be contacted.

**What it includes:**

- preprocessing and leakage handling
- XGBoost modeling
- top-k targeting policy simulation
- reusable scoring logic
- SQLite score storage
- Streamlit app
- FastAPI prediction endpoint
- Tableau dashboard

**Selected result:** a Top 20% targeting policy captured 64.78% of subscribers in the historical test set, with a 37.77% subscription rate inside the recommended group and 3.24x lift over the baseline.

Repository: [bank-campaign-targeting-system](https://github.com/Rlvtick/bank-campaign-targeting-system)

### Credit Risk Classification

Credit risk modeling project developed from a project-based internship case.

The project focuses on detecting high-risk borrowers while avoiding data leakage from post-loan information. The main goal is not just model accuracy, but risk ranking that can support manual review prioritization.

**What it includes:**

- loan default classification
- leakage-safe feature selection
- imbalanced classification handling
- XGBoost modeling
- ROC-AUC, recall, and KS evaluation
- business interpretation for risk review policy

**Selected result:** tuned XGBoost reached 0.720 ROC-AUC, identified 67% of bad loans, and concentrated risk 2.28x higher than average in the top 10% riskiest applicants.

Repository: [Credit-Risk-Classification-idx](https://github.com/Rlvtick/Credit-Risk-Classification-idx)
