## About Me

I'm a Data Science undergraduate from Universitas Airlangga, Indonesia, building projects around machine learning, analytics, dashboards, and practical decision-support systems.

My work sits between **data science** and **business analytics**: I like taking messy data, turning it into usable features, testing models properly, and packaging the results into something people can actually use, such as dashboards, scoring tools, APIs, and reports.

Right now, I am focused on building stronger end-to-end data projects in financial analytics, risk modeling, customer targeting, data quality, and operational intelligence.

I am open to remote work and relocation opportunities, especially across Europe and Asia.

## Featured Data Projects

<table>
  <tr>
    <td width="50%">
      <h3>Bank Campaign Targeting System</h3>
      <p>
        End-to-end machine learning project for prioritizing bank marketing campaign targets.
        Instead of treating the model as a simple yes/no classifier, this project ranks customers
        by predicted subscription likelihood and evaluates which top percentage of customers should be contacted.
      </p>
      <p>
        <b>Includes:</b> preprocessing, leakage handling, XGBoost modeling, top-k policy simulation,
        SQLite storage, Streamlit app, FastAPI endpoint, and Tableau dashboard.
      </p>
      <p>
        <b>Selected result:</b> Top 20% targeting policy captured 64.78% of subscribers in the historical test set,
        with 37.77% subscription rate inside the recommended group and 3.24x lift over baseline.
      </p>
      <p>
        <b>Stack:</b> Python, XGBoost, SQLite, Streamlit, FastAPI, Tableau
      </p>

    <td width="50%">
      <h3>Credit Risk Classification</h3>
      <p>
        Credit risk modeling project focused on detecting high-risk borrowers while avoiding data leakage
        from post-loan information.
      </p>
      <p>
        The goal is not just model accuracy, but risk ranking that can support manual review prioritization.
      </p>
      <p>
        <b>Selected result:</b> Tuned XGBoost reached 0.720 ROC-AUC, identified 67% of bad loans,
        and concentrated risk 2.28x higher than average in the top 10% riskiest applicants.
      </p>
      <p>
        <b>Stack:</b> Python, pandas, scikit-learn, XGBoost, model evaluation
      </p>
